Resolution Audit

Estimate effective PPI from actual source pixels and printed placement. Calculations run locally in your browser.

Check the actual pixels.

Enter source pixel dimensions and the physical size the image will occupy on the printed page—not the entire paper size unless the image fills it.

What the result means

Effective PPI divides actual source pixels by the final physical size in inches. The lower of the two axes is the conservative result.

This tool cannot inspect real image sharpness, compression, sharpening or printer capabilities.

Read effective PPI explained and large format viewing distance.

Vendor specifications first.

Results are estimates, not print shop approval. Verify tolerances, binding, finishing and file requirements before placing an order.
