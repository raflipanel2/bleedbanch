Sheet Yield Estimator

Estimate a simple rectangular layout and required press sheets. Calculations run locally in your browser.

Estimate pieces per sheet.

Simple rectangular grid math. Assumes a uniform layout orientation and cannot model die cuts, printer gripper areas, variable layouts or finishing constraints.

What the result means

The estimator compares all pieces in one orientation versus rotating all pieces 90°. It does not solve mixed-orientation nesting.

Gaps and margins are simplified geometric allowances, not universal minimum requirements. Ask your printer for gripper and gutter specifications.

Related: business cards and stickers and die cuts.

Vendor specifications first.

Results are estimates, not print shop approval. Verify tolerances, binding, finishing and file requirements before placing an order.
