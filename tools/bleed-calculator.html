Bleed Builder

Dimension calculator for trim, full bleed and safe content zones. Calculations run locally in your browser.

Start with the trim.

Choose a familiar format or enter your own dimensions in millimeters. Bleed is applied on each edge; safe margin sits inside the trim.

What the result means

Trim: the final cut size.

Bleed: background artwork beyond the trim.

Safe margin: inside area for important text and graphics.

Pixels: estimated raster canvas dimensions at selected PPI. PDF/vector content is not limited by these numbers.

Try the three-boundary guide and paper size guide.

Vendor specifications first.

Results are estimates, not print shop approval. Verify tolerances, binding, finishing and file requirements before placing an order.
