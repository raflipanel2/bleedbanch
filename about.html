THE DETAILS

About BleedBench

What this independent educational print-prep website does, who it is for and what it does not claim.

The purpose

BleedBench is an independent educational project built to make print preparation more understandable for designers, small businesses and students. The site focuses on questions that arise before sending artwork to a print provider: final size, bleed, safe areas, resolution, PDF export, finishing and proofing.

What makes the guides practical

Articles use concrete dimensions, examples, failure modes and short checklists. The interactive tools show how their calculations work and label their limitations. Neither the articles nor the tools are intended to certify files or replace print-shop preflight.

What we are not

BleedBench is not a print manufacturer, accredited production inspector, color-measurement laboratory or licensed design service. We do not claim to have tested every printer, paper stock or export preset. References are linked so readers can verify technical statements in original manufacturer guidance.

Corrections and contact

If you spot an error, a broken link or a confusing calculation, email raflipanel2@gmail.com with the page address, the issue and a source where possible. See our editorial standards and source directory.
