Print resolution in 2026: why context beats a fixed PPI rule

A reference note based on Adobe’s February 2026 Photoshop print resolution guidance.

BASED ON OFFICIAL DOCUMENTATIONLAST VERIFIED 09 NOV 2026

What the current documentation says

Adobe’s Photoshop print-resolution reference, updated 23 February 2026, treats 300 PPI as a common close-view print standard while acknowledging that large-format graphics viewed from a distance may work at lower resolution. That distinction matters more than a generic DPI label.

Calculate from real pixels

A 3000-pixel-wide image has 300 effective PPI at 10 inches wide. At 20 inches, the same source has 150 PPI. The relationship is mathematical and does not change because the document metadata has a different resolution value.

Use source quality and process as context

Compression, focus, sharpening, printing surface and viewing distance can change the apparent quality. Vector illustrations stay sharp for many geometric details but raster photographs still depend on pixel dimensions.

How we apply this note

BleedBench’s Resolution Audit reports effective PPI, then asks readers to confirm requirements with their chosen print provider. We checked the source on 9 November 2026 and do not present a universal pass/fail guarantee for every print technology.
